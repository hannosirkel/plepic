# Review of the site-translation plan

Independent review of [`plan.md`](./plan.md) and [`tasks.json`](./tasks.json)
against the operator's brief of 2026-10-10 and the repository at commit
`be42218`. Reviewed on 2026-10-10. The files under review were not edited.

Every claim below was checked against the repository, the published PDF, the
read-only inputs or the tooling on this machine; the Evidence column says how.
Severity is `must-fix` when the plan or a task would fail an acceptance
criterion, break a stated rule of the plan, or let a requirement go unmet, and
`optional` otherwise.
