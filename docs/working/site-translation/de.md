# German translation conventions

## Address and register

Use **du** in marketing copy and the rulebook, including imperatives. Use **Sie** in legal pages and checkout acknowledgements. Prefer neutral references such as „die Spielenden“ or „alle“ where they read naturally; avoid changing a named character or a rule's meaning to do so.

## Typography and numbers

Use German quotation marks („…“) for quotations and ordinary quoted terms. Keep English card titles and Station names exactly as printed, without translating their spelling or capitalisation. Use the German decimal comma and thousands point (1,5; 1.000); write prices as 19,90 €. Use day, month, year dates (12.10.2026 in compact UI, 12. Oktober 2026 in prose). Keep rulebook game quantities as digits where the English rulebook uses digits.

## Game terms in running text

Follow `content/glossary/de.ts`. Translate game components and action names, then add the English card label in parentheses at the first use on each page or rulebook sheet: „Auslage (Supply)“, „Hauptaktion (Main Action)“. Subsequent uses on that page or sheet use the German term alone. A card title such as *Double Agent* and a Station name such as *Terran Outpost* stay in English, exactly as printed. Write action names as German infinitives in headings (for example „Ziehen (Draw)“) and inflect the German verb in instructions („Ziehe 1 Karte“). Retain the English action name only at first use. Distinguish the Nachziehstapel from the Ablagestapel.

## Rulebook examples

The English sentences below are from `rulebook/text/en.ts`; the German lines demonstrate the glossary. Parentheses mark first use on a page or sheet.

1. `s06.f03` — “Each player draws 3 cards from the top of the deck into their hand. These are kept secret from the other players.”
   „Alle ziehen 3 Karten vom Nachziehstapel auf die Hand. Halte diese Karten vor den anderen geheim.“
2. `s06.f04` — “Deal 3 cards plus 1 additional card per player from the deck. Place these face-up between the players to form the Supply.”
   „Gib 3 Karten und 1 weitere Karte pro Person vom Nachziehstapel aus. Lege sie offen zwischen die Spielenden; sie bilden die Auslage (Supply).“
3. `s06.f05` — “Give each player a Credit Counter, set to 3 Credits. Use the Credit Counter to track your total Credits.”
   „Gib allen einen Creditzähler und stelle ihn auf 3 Credits (Credit). Mit dem Creditzähler hältst du die Gesamtzahl deiner Credits fest.“
4. `s07.f04` — “Each player takes a turn, beginning with the starting player and proceeding clockwise. This continues until a player wins by achieving one or more victory conditions.”
   „Alle kommen reihum im Uhrzeigersinn zum Zug, beginnend mit der Startperson. Das geht so weiter, bis jemand mindestens eine Siegbedingung erfüllt und gewinnt.“
5. `s07.f10` — “Colonists and scientific achievements appear on Station and Module cards. Credits are gained by various means. Influences are a special card type for advanced players.”
   „Kolonistinnen und Kolonisten sowie wissenschaftliche Errungenschaften erscheinen auf Stationskarten (Station) und Modulkarten (Module). Credits (Credit) erhältst du auf verschiedene Weise. Einflusskarten (Influence) sind ein besonderer Kartentyp für Fortgeschrittene.“
