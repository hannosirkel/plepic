# Q6: a prevailing-language clause on translated legal pages

Opened 2026-10-12, when the site-translation plan was retired. The operator
has not answered this question. The reading below is in force.

The terms of sale, the returns page and the other legal pages now exist in
four languages. The brief approved every translation in advance. It does not
say whether a translated page must state which language version prevails if
two differ.

## Reading in force

No clause is added. Each translated legal page mirrors the English page
section for section, and `content/mirror.test.ts` holds it to the same
anchors, placeholders and coverage.

## Why it is still open

This is a legal question. The German and Klingon legal pages are marked
`operator-approved` on the strength of the operator's advance approval of
2026-10-10, and no separate reading of their wording is recorded. The
Estonian pages carry a qualified reader's acceptance, confirmed on
2026-08-20. None of the three readings covered a prevailing-language clause.

A consumer reads the contract in the language of the edition they ordered
from. Without a clause, each version stands on its own.

## What closing it takes

The operator, with the qualified reviewer, decides whether a clause is wanted
and which version prevails. A clause is one new sentence in the terms page of
each edition. It lands in `content/legal/terms.ts` and its three mirrors
together, because the mirror test compares their structure.
