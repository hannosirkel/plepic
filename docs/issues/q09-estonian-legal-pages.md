# Q9: whether the Estonian legal pages are translated again

Opened 2026-10-12, when the site-translation plan was retired. The operator
has not answered this question. The reading below is in force.

The Estonian edition had its five legal pages before the plan began. They
were translated and approved earlier. The brief asks for the site in
Estonian and does not say whether those pages are redone.

## Reading in force

No. The five pages in `content/legal/et/` stand as approved. The Estonian
glossary was written to agree with them, not the other way round.

## Where it shows

[`docs/current/translation/et.md`](../current/translation/et.md), section
Terms the legal pages set, lists the terms those pages fixed. Three strings
of those pages differ from what the glossary would ask of new copy. They are
named in `glossaryExceptions` in `content/glossary/index.ts`, each with its
reason.

## If the answer differs

A new translation of the five pages removes the three exceptions and needs a
new reading. The pages are `operator-approved` today on a qualified reader's
acceptance of the Estonian text, confirmed on 2026-08-20.
